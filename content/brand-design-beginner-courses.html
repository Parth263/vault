1 · Basics (weeks 1–4): Davio White's Brand Identity Design: From Sketch to Handoff (self-paced, 6k). Learn the full process once, end to end.

3 · Live feedback + AI workflow: Ajala Victor's AVmazing Brand Identity Design + AI (recorded class now, live in April 2027) or the next Bimbo Oni Studio cohort with @dean_maj.
4 · Stay current on AI tools: Flux Membership (AI sprints, 'Stand Out Branding') or Safwan's AI-integrated Logo & Brand Design Course.
5 · Sell it: CREATIVEMAVIX's mentorship covers proposals, pitching and turning logo-only jobs into full identity work.
