⚖️ Framer vs custom code (Jun–Oct 2026)

Verdict: Framer client work is not dead. Framer template income is down, and code is the premium tier.
Founders and studios still post asks for Framer designers. What dropped is marketplace template sales. The move to code is real, but mostly teams moving their own Webflow sites with Claude Code. Agencies stay stack-agnostic ("depends on the client"). No beginner in this research landed a first client with a coded site; the documented first clients were Framer or design jobs.

🧭 What this means for you

1 · Get the first client the fast way
Sell 'landing page design + build' as an outcome. Pick the stack per client: Framer if they want to edit it themselves, Next.js if they need app features or own-the-code. Same price.
2 · Use your edge: frontend + Claude
Pitch yourself as a design engineer: 'designed and coded with Claude Code, you own the repo'. That's where the $150–250K jobs and Bengaluru roles are. The rarest skill is taste plus shipping, not prompting.
3 · Stop relying on template income
Template sales are falling. Use templates only as lead magnets with a 'customise for your brand' offer. Don't count on them as income.
4 · Avoid the AI-sameness trap
Clients can get an average Claude site for free. Win on positioning, copy and motion that AI defaults don't produce. Post the why behind each design.
5 · This week
Rebuild one of your Framer templates in Next.js with Claude Code. Post a side-by-side (Framer vs code, time, cost) with the hook 'I build in both. Here's when each wins.'
